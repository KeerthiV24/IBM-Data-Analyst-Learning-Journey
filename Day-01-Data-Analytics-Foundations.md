## 🧠 Key Concepts Learned

### 1. Introduction to Data Analytics
Understood the fundamentals of data analytics and how organizations use data to generate insights and make informed decisions.

### 2. Data Analyst Career Paths
Explored different career opportunities in the data analytics field, including Data Analyst, Business Analyst, Business Intelligence Analyst, Data Scientist, and Data Engineer.

### 3. Responsibilities of a Data Analyst
Learned about the key responsibilities of a data analyst, including understanding business problems, collecting data, preparing data, analyzing information, and communicating findings.

### 4. Types of Data Analytics

- **Descriptive Analytics:** What happened?
- **Diagnostic Analytics:** Why did it happen?
- **Predictive Analytics:** What might happen?
- **Prescriptive Analytics:** What should we do?

### 5. Data Analytics Process
Learned the six stages of the data analytics process:

1. Ask
2. Prepare
3. Process
4. Analyze
5. Share
6. Act

### 6. Types of Data

- Structured Data
- Semi-Structured Data
- Unstructured Data

Also explored data repositories and different data sources.

---

## 💡 Key Takeaways

- Data analytics helps businesses make informed decisions.
- Data analysts identify patterns and generate meaningful insights.
- Understanding business problems is an important part of analytics.
- Different analytical approaches answer different business questions.
- Data preparation and processing are important stages of analysis.
