# Day 06 – Statistical Analysis, Data Mining and Data Visualization

**Date:** 13 November 2026  
**Course:** IBM Data Analyst Professional Certificate

## 📚 Topics Covered 

### 1. Overview of Statistical Analysis
- Statistics is a branch of mathematics used for collecting, analyzing, interpreting, and presenting numerical data.
- Statistical analysis applies statistical methods to a sample of data to understand what the data represents.
- Helps in making informed decisions and identifying meaningful relationships.

### 2. Population and Sample
- **Population:** The complete group of people or things being studied.
- **Sample:** A representative selection taken from the population.

### 3. Types of Statistics

**Descriptive Statistics**
- Summarizes and presents data in a meaningful way.
- Helps understand the main characteristics of a dataset.
- Does not draw conclusions about the entire population.

**Common Measures:**

- **Mean:** Average value.
- **Median:** Middle value.
- **Mode:** Most frequently occurring value.
- **Variance:** Measures how spread out data values are.
- **Standard Deviation:** Measures how closely data is distributed around the mean.
- **Range:** Difference between the smallest and largest values.
- **Skewness:** Measures the asymmetry of a data distribution.

**Inferential Statistics**
- Uses sample data to make conclusions about a larger population.
- Helps apply sample findings to the population.

**Common Methods:**
- Hypothesis Testing
- Confidence Intervals
- Regression Analysis

### 4. Data Mining
- Data mining is the process of extracting useful knowledge and patterns from data.
- Uses pattern recognition, statistical analysis, and mathematical techniques.
- Helps identify relationships, trends, and variations in data.

**Applications of Data Mining:**
- Understanding customer behaviour.
- Detecting fraudulent financial transactions.
- Predicting student performance.
- Supporting medical decisions.
- Identifying crime patterns.
- Matching supply and logistics with demand forecasts.

### 5. Data Mining Techniques

- **Classification:** Assigns data into predefined categories.
- **Clustering:** Groups similar data into clusters.
- **Anomaly Detection:** Identifies unusual or unexpected patterns.
- **Association Rule Mining:** Finds relationships between different data items.
- **Sequential Pattern Mining:** Identifies patterns in a sequence of events.
- **Affinity Grouping:** Discovers items or events that frequently occur together.
- **Decision Trees:** Uses a tree structure to support classification and predictions.
- **Regression:** Identifies relationships between variables.

### 6. Data Mining Tools
- Microsoft Excel and spreadsheets.
- R Programming.
- Python.
- IBM SPSS Statistics.
- IBM Watson Studio.
- SAS.

### 7. Overview of Communicating and Sharing Data Analysis Findings

**Data Analysis Process:**
- Understand the problem.
- Communicate the findings.

**Data Projects Involve:**
- Collaboration across business functions.
- People with multidisciplinary skills.
- Incorporating findings into business decisions.

**Before Communicating, Ask:**
- Who is my audience?
- What information is important to them?
- What will help them take action?

**Structuring a Presentation:**
- Review your data.
- State your assumptions.
- Organize your presentation.
- Identify the best format for presenting data.

### 8. Data Storytelling
- Data storytelling combines data, visualization, and communication.
- Helps explain findings in a clear and meaningful way.
- Supports better understanding and decision-making.
- A good data story balances simplicity with the complexity of the data.

### 9. Introduction to Data Visualization
- Data visualization communicates information using visual elements.
- Examples include graphs, charts, and maps.
- Makes information easier to understand, interpret, and relate to.

**Choosing the Right Visualization:**
- Identify the relationship you want to show.
- Decide whether you need to compare values.
- Determine whether you want to show correlations.
- Identify whether you need to detect anomalies.
- Consider what your audience needs to understand.

### 10. Common Types of Charts

- **Bar Chart:** Compares different categories or datasets.
- **Column Chart:** Compares values side by side and shows changes over time.
- **Pie Chart:** Shows proportions of categories as parts of a whole.
- **Line Chart:** Displays trends and changes over time.
- **Dashboard:** Combines reports and visualizations from multiple data sources in one graphical interface.

**Benefits of Dashboards:**
- Easy to understand.
- Provide an overall view of important information.
- Support collaboration between teams.
- Help generate reports and monitor performance.

## 💡 Key Takeaways
- Statistics helps summarize and understand data.
- Descriptive and inferential statistics serve different purposes.
- Data mining helps discover useful patterns and relationships.
- Data storytelling makes analytical findings easier to communicate.
- Choosing the right chart improves data understanding.
- Dashboards bring important information together in one place.
- Visualization tools help present data effectively.
